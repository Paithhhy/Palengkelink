# Proposed System: PalengkeLink

### System Name
**PalengkeLink: Public Market Vendor, Stall, and Supply Management System**

### Functionalities

- **Vendor Management:** Register and manage vendor information and business details.
- **Stall Management:** Manage stall assignments, stall availability, and market sections.
- **Rental Payment Tracking:** Record monthly rental payments, payment history, and unpaid balances.
- **Product and Supply Management:** Manage products sold by vendors and track their suppliers and supply transactions.
- **Inspection Management:** Record market inspections, sanitation checks, and violations.
- **Maintenance Requests:** Allow vendors to report damaged stalls or other market problems.
- **Reports and Dashboard:** Display vendor records, occupied and vacant stalls, payment reports, and inspection results.

### Possible Users and Their Capabilities

**1. Admin / Market Manager**
- Manage all vendor and stall records.
- Assign vendors to stalls.
- Monitor rental payments and unpaid balances.
- Manage user accounts and generate reports.

**2. Market Staff / Inspector**
- View assigned vendor and stall information.
- Record sanitation inspections and violations.
- Update stall status and submit maintenance reports.

**3. Vendor**
- View their assigned stall and rental information.
- View and manage their product and supply records.
- Check payment history.
- Submit maintenance requests.

### Problem the System Solves

Many public markets still rely on manual records, paper receipts, and separate files to manage vendors, stalls, and rental payments. This can cause difficulty in tracking vacant stalls, unpaid rent, inspection records, and vendor information. PalengkeLink solves this problem by providing a centralized system that organizes market operations, reduces duplicate records, and makes information easier to access. It also helps market administrators monitor payments, stall assignments, and market activities more efficiently.


### Additional System Feature

The system can support multiple public market branches, where each branch has its own vendors, stalls, rental records, and staff, while the main administrator can monitor the overall operations.
